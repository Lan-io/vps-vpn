## ⚠️ Honest Drawbacks

### 1. Not the Cheapest Option

If your priority is the absolute lowest price, LisaHost may not be your best bet. Dual-ISP residential IP products start at ¥68/month — noticeably higher than ¥20-30 VPS providers offering standard datacenter IPs. However, this is a cost difference driven by IP quality, not arbitrary markup.

### 2. Occasional Network Incidents

The UK datacenter experienced service disruptions when an upstream provider reclaimed IP ranges. LisaHost migrated to Sky Telecom residential IPs and compensated affected users with multiple times the remaining service period. Such operational risks exist objectively, but the response was reasonably responsible.

### 3. Randomized SSH Ports

New instances are assigned random SSH ports (not port 22) by default, designed to prevent brute-force attacks and maintain IP cleanliness. Beginners need to check their control panel for the port, adding a slight learning curve.

### 4. Occasional Congestion on China Mobile US Routes

38-segment and 154-segment ISP residential IPs experience congestion for China Mobile users on US-bound routes. China Telecom and China Unicom users are unaffected. Mobile users should consider 70-segment/64-segment alternatives.
